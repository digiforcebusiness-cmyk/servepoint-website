Privacy Policy

What the Tilawa Quran app does with your data on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Summary

  • Tilawa has no account or sign-up. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting tools in the app.
  • Tilawa never records or saves your voice. Voice following uses your device’s own speech recognition service.
  • Your bookmarks, reading positions and settings stay on your device.

2. Data stored on your device

The following is saved only in the app’s local storage on your device. It is never sent to us:

  • Bookmarks and the last page you read, for each riwayah (Hafs and Warsh)
  • Settings such as theme, colours, font size, layout, translation language, chosen reciter and hidden-verse mode

Deleting the app, or clearing its data in your device settings, removes all of it.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

Voice following and memorization practice are optional. They start only when you tap the microphone and grant the Microphone permission (and, on iOS, the Speech Recognition permission).

  • iPhone and iPad: your speech is converted to text by Apple’s Speech framework. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process the audio on its servers, under Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Android: your speech is converted to text by the speech recognition service installed on your device, usually Google’s, which may process the audio online under Google’s Privacy Policy.

Tilawa receives only the recognized text, uses it to highlight or reveal the words you recite, and keeps it in memory while the session runs. It is not saved, uploaded or shared by Tilawa. The microphone is never used in the background.

4. Audio recitations

When you play a recitation, the reciter list and audio files are streamed from mp3quran.net. Like any website, that server receives your device’s IP address and standard request information in order to deliver the audio. Tilawa sends it no other information about you.

5. Data shared with third parties

We share no data with anyone. The only external services involved are the ones described above: your device’s speech recognition provider (Apple or Google) when you use voice following, and mp3quran.net when you stream audio.

6. Children

Tilawa is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

All network connections made by the app use encrypted HTTPS. The Quran text, translations and fonts are included in the app, so reading works without internet.

8. Your rights and data deletion

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You can remove everything Tilawa stores by deleting the app or clearing its data. For any privacy question, write to digiforcebusiness@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If Tilawa’s handling of data changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.

10. Contact

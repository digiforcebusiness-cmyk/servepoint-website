Frequently asked questions

How do I follow my recitation by voice?
Open the page you want to recite, tap the microphone and allow Microphone (and Speech Recognition on iPhone). Start from the beginning of the page. The word you are reciting is highlighted and the page follows you.
How do I test my memorization?
Turn on hidden verses, then tap the microphone and recite from memory. Each word appears as you say it. Press and hold a hidden word to peek at it if you get stuck.
Voice following does not react or stops.
Check that you have an internet connection and that Arabic is available in your device’s speech recognition settings (on Android, in the Google speech services or dictation settings). Recite clearly from the start of the visible page. Tap the microphone notice to see what your device heard, and include a screenshot of it if you contact us.
Does Tilawa grade my tajweed?
No. Voice following recognizes which words you recite. It does not assess pronunciation, tajweed rules or harakat.
Which riwayat are available?
Hafs ʿan ʿAsim and Warsh ʿan Nafiʿ, each with its own text, verse numbering and King Fahd Complex font. Switch between them from the app’s menu; bookmarks and reading positions are kept separately for each.
Does the app work offline?
Yes. The Quran text, translations, search and bookmarks work without internet. Streaming recitations and, on most devices, voice following need a connection.
I found an error in the text or a translation.
Please tell us using the form above (subject “Error in the Quran text or a translation”), with the riwayah, surah and verse.
How do I delete my data?
Tilawa has no account and sends no personal data to us. Your bookmarks and settings are stored only on your device; deleting the app removes them. See the privacy policy.

Privacy Policy

Real Tractor Farm Simulator

Last updated: July 12, 2026

The Short Version

Real Tractor Farm Simulator does not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no login, and no tracking of you by us. Your farm, money, progress, and settings are stored only on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and it contains no in-app purchases.

1. Who We Are

Real Tractor Farm Simulator ("the game", "we", "our") is a mobile farming game published by DigiForce. This Privacy Policy explains what data the game does and does not collect when you play it on iOS.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Account credentials of any kind (the game has no sign-in)
  • Location data
  • Photos, contacts, or device files
  • Microphone or camera input
  • Your gameplay analytics or crash reports on our own servers

The game has no servers, no databases, and no telemetry of its own. We never see how you play.

3. Data Stored Only on Your Device

So your farm continues between sessions, the game saves the following information locally. This data never leaves your device:

  • Money and progression — your cash balance, unlocked fields, and star ratings
  • Owned machines — the tractors, plows, seeders, harvesters and paint scheme you bought or selected
  • Farm state — your animals, orchard trees, hired workers, and the timestamps used to calculate offline earnings
  • Daily content — your current orders/contracts and their progress
  • Settings — sound on/off and similar preferences

This is stored in the device's standard local storage (PlayerPrefs on iOS). You can clear it at any time through Settings → Reset Progress inside the game, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Advertising

Real Tractor Farm Simulator is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob is the only third-party service the game uses. The game displays:

  • A banner ad during gameplay
  • Occasional full-screen interstitial ads between levels (with a cooldown)
  • Optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for bonus cash, doubled earnings, or instant crop growth

To serve, measure, and frequency-cap ads, AdMob may collect a limited set of device signals such as device type, IP address, an advertising identifier, and ad-request metadata. Depending on your consent and region, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. For full details of AdMob's data practices, see Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

On iOS, you can control app tracking system-wide via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and limit ad personalization via Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

5. In-App Purchases

The game contains no in-app purchases. Everything in the game is earned by playing. No payment information is ever requested or processed.

6. Children's Privacy

Real Tractor Farm Simulator is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the game has no login, no chat, no social features, and no in-app purchases, and because we operate no servers, no personal data is gathered from any player.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions, please contact us at the email address below.

7. Permissions the App Requests

The game requests only the minimum permissions needed to function:

  • Internet access — used only so AdMob can fetch and display ads. The game itself plays fully offline.
  • Vibration — for haptic feedback during play (for example, on completing a harvest).

The game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, calendar, or any other sensitive data.

8. Your Rights

Because we do not collect any personal information on our servers, there is no data about you for us to access, correct, or delete remotely. You retain full control over the on-device data described in Section 3:

  • Reset your progress: Settings → Reset Progress (inside the game)
  • Delete all game data: uninstall the app from your device

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California, and other jurisdictions with privacy laws granting rights of access, correction, deletion, and objection may exercise those rights at any time by contacting us. Because we hold no personal data, most requests will be answered by confirming that no such data exists.

9. Data Retention

We retain no user data because we collect none. On-device data persists only as long as the game remains installed, and is removed when you uninstall the app or choose "Reset Progress".

10. International Users

Real Tractor Farm Simulator is available worldwide. The game does not transfer personal data across borders because it does not collect any. Ads served by Google AdMob may be delivered from Google's global infrastructure and are subject to Google's own privacy policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes affecting how data is handled will be highlighted. Continued use of the game after a change indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

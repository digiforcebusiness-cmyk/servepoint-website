Common questions

How do I reset my farm? Open Settings inside the game and choose "Reset Progress". This clears your money, fields, and farm from the device.

Is my progress backed up? Progress is saved locally on your device. There are no accounts, so uninstalling the app removes your saved farm.

How do offline earnings work? Owned fields earn rent while you're away, and hired workers keep your animals and orchard producing. A welcome-back chest shows the total when you return.

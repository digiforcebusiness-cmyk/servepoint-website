Frequently asked

Is Real Tractor Farm Simulator free?

Yes — the whole game is free to play with no in-app purchases. All 100 fields, every machine, animals, the orchard, workers and paint schemes are earned by playing. The game shows ads from Google AdMob to support development; watching an optional rewarded ad can grant bonus cash or double your earnings, but it's always your choice.

Does it need internet?

No. All driving, farming, and progress work fully offline. An internet connection is only used to load ads.

Do I need an account?

No accounts, no sign-up, no login. Your farm and progress are saved locally on your device. See the privacy policy for details.

What happens while I'm away?

Your owned fields earn passive rent, and if you've hired a Herder or Orchardist your animals and trees keep producing. When you come back, a welcome-back chest shows everything your farm earned while you were gone.

When will Android launch?

An Android version is planned. Email digiforcebusiness@gmail.com if you'd like to be notified at launch.

Found a bug or have an idea?

Email digiforcebusiness@gmail.com with a quick description (and a screenshot if it's visual). We read every message and reply within a week.

Ask us a question ↗
How much does ServePoint cost?

ServePoint Pro is $9.99 USD per month, with unlimited devices, kitchen display, inventory, reports and receipt printing included. Check your app store for the price and subscription terms in your region.

Which devices can I use?

ServePoint is available for Android, iPhone, iPad and Windows. Choose your store below to check the supported operating system versions before installing.

Can waiters and the kitchen use separate devices?

Yes. A waiter can take an order on a phone or tablet and send it to the kitchen display. Connected devices sync orders in real time. An internet connection is needed for live synchronization.

Does it work with a receipt printer?

ServePoint supports 58mm and 80mm Bluetooth thermal printers. Contact us with your printer model and operating system to check compatibility before purchasing hardware.

Can I explore the app before subscribing?

You can install ServePoint and explore the app before starting a Pro subscription. Review the subscription details in the app; downloading the app does not mean that the Pro plan is free.

Which languages does the app support?

English, French, Spanish and Arabic, including right-to-left support for Arabic.

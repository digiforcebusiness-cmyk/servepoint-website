Delete your AdGen AI account

How to delete your account and the data associated with it

Option 1: Delete in the app

The fastest way. Your account is deleted immediately.

  1. Open AdGen AI and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete.

Option 2: Request deletion by web

If you no longer have the app installed, submit this form using the email address linked to your AdGen AI account. We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days. You can also email digiforcebusiness@gmail.com with the subject "AdGen AI account deletion".

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email/password or Google sign-in link)
  • Your profile and remaining credit balance
  • Videos and images you uploaded and the videos you generated
  • Your generation history

What is retained

  • Purchase records: transaction records held by Google Play and our payment processor are kept as required for tax, accounting and fraud prevention (up to 7 years).
  • Backups: data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before being overwritten.

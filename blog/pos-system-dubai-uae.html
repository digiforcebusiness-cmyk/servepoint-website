POS System in Dubai & UAE: Best Restaurant POS Software (2026)

Dubai and the wider UAE are among the most competitive restaurant markets in the world — high rents, demanding guests and thin margins mean the right POS system pays for itself fast. But UAE restaurants also have specific needs a generic POS ignores: 5% VAT handling, bilingual Arabic + English service, and reliability across multiple branches. Here's what to look for in a POS system in Dubai, and how ServePoint fits.

What a POS system needs for the UAE

VAT-ready billing. The UAE applies 5% VAT. Your POS should apply your tax rate automatically and print clear, itemized tax receipts every time — no manual maths, no inconsistent bills.

Arabic and English. Staff and guests span both languages. A POS that runs in Arabic (with proper right-to-left layout) and English — and prints receipts accordingly — serves Dubai's mixed workforce and clientele far better.

Reliable connectivity. Whether it's a mall unit or a beach-side kiosk, signal varies. Before you commit to a cloud POS, test what still works when the connection drops.

Multi-branch ready. Many UAE operators run several outlets. Real-time cloud sync lets you see every branch's sales from your phone.

Why "cheap" beats "big" for most UAE restaurants

Enterprise POS suites in the region often bundle expensive hardware and per-terminal licences that make sense for a large chain but bleed an independent café or a single-branch restaurant. For most UAE operators, a modern app-based POS that runs on tablets you already own — with a flat monthly fee and no per-transaction cut — delivers the same core capability at a fraction of the cost. See our guide to the best POS system for restaurants.

Hardware: keep it simple

You don't need a proprietary Dubai "POS terminal package." An Android or iOS tablet or phone (or a Windows PC) plus a Bluetooth thermal printer is enough to take orders and print receipts — and it's easy to add a second device for a busy service or a new branch.

Before you buy a POS in Dubai: five questions

  1. Does it show 5% VAT correctly on your receipts, and do those receipts meet your accountant's requirements?
  2. Does it support any current Federal Tax Authority or e-invoicing rules that apply to your business?
  3. Can staff switch between Arabic and English, and do printed receipts look right in both?
  4. What does the quote include for each extra branch, terminal and kitchen screen?
  5. Who supports you locally, and in which time zone and languages?

POS System in Dubai & UAE: Best Restaurant POS Software (2026)

Dubai and the wider UAE are among the most competitive restaurant markets in the world — high rents, demanding guests and thin margins mean the right POS system pays for itself fast. But UAE restaurants also have specific needs a generic POS ignores: 5% VAT handling, bilingual Arabic + English service, and reliability across multiple branches. Here's what to look for in a POS system in Dubai, and how ServePoint fits.

What a POS system needs for the UAE

VAT-ready billing. The UAE applies 5% VAT. Your POS should apply your tax rate automatically and print clear, itemized tax receipts every time — no manual maths, no inconsistent bills.

Arabic and English. Staff and guests span both languages. A POS that runs in Arabic (with proper right-to-left layout) and English — and prints receipts accordingly — serves Dubai's mixed workforce and clientele far better.

Offline reliability. Whether it's a mall unit or a beach-side kiosk, connectivity varies. An offline-first POS keeps selling and printing through any drop, then syncs automatically.

Multi-branch ready. Many UAE operators run several outlets. Real-time cloud sync lets you see every branch's sales from your phone.

Why "cheap" beats "big" for most UAE restaurants

Enterprise POS suites in the region often bundle expensive hardware and per-terminal licences that make sense for a large chain but bleed an independent café or a single-branch restaurant. For most UAE operators, a modern app-based POS that runs on tablets you already own — with a flat monthly fee and no per-transaction cut — delivers the same core capability at a fraction of the cost. Hardware: keep it simple

You don't need a proprietary Dubai "POS terminal package." An Android or iOS tablet or phone (or a Windows PC) plus a Bluetooth thermal printer is enough to take orders, bill with VAT and print receipts — and it's easy to add a second device for a busy service or a new branch.

Restaurant Billing Software: Features, Pricing & How to Choose (2026)

Restaurant billing software is the system that turns an order into a bill — it prices items, adds taxes and discounts, prints the receipt and records the sale. In practice it's the same tool as a restaurant POS, just named for the part owners care about most: fast, accurate billing. This guide covers what it does, the features that actually matter, what it should cost, and how to choose the right one for your restaurant or café.

What restaurant billing software does

Good restaurant billing software handles the whole billing cycle without slowing your staff down:

Itemized bills in seconds. Tap the items, apply a discount or service charge, split the bill if needed, and total it instantly — no mental math, no mistakes.

Taxes and receipts. It applies your tax rules automatically and prints a clean, itemized receipt (or shares a digital one), so every bill is consistent and professional.

Payments. Cash, card and mixed payments, with automatic change calculation.

A record of every sale. Each bill is saved and rolled into sales reports so you can see revenue, best-sellers and peak hours.

Billing software vs. a full POS — are they different?

Not really. "Restaurant billing software" and "restaurant POS system" describe the same product from two angles: billing software emphasizes the invoice/receipt, a POS emphasizes the whole front-of-house. The best tools do both — plus send orders to the kitchen display and track inventory. When you shop, don't pay for a "billing-only" tool that can't grow with you.

The features that actually matter

Speed at the counter. Billing happens dozens of times an hour — the interface has to be fast and forgiving.

Works offline. Billing can't stop because the internet dropped. Choose software that keeps billing and printing offline, then syncs to the cloud automatically.

Thermal receipt printing. Support for 58mm and 80mm Bluetooth thermal printers so you print bills without cables.

Runs on hardware you own. Android/iOS phones, tablets or a Windows PC beat locked-in billing machines.

Reports built in. Daily and monthly totals, taxes collected and top items — without exporting to a spreadsheet.

How much should restaurant billing software cost?

Prices swing from "free" apps (that recover the cost through card fees or paid add-ons) to per-terminal licenses at $50–$200+ per month. For most independent restaurants and cafés, a flat monthly fee with unlimited devices is the cheapest route once you run more than one billing point — you're not paying again for every screen. Watch for per-transaction fees; at billing volumes they quietly become your biggest cost.

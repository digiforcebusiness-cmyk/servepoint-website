The Best Loyverse Alternative for Restaurants (2026)

Loyverse is a popular free POS app, and for good reason — it's easy to start with and costs nothing to install. But many restaurant owners start looking for an alternative once the "free" plan meets its limits: paid monthly add-ons per store, and a feature set that leans more toward retail than a busy kitchen. If that's you, here's an honest look at what to compare — and how ServePoint fits.

Why people look for a Loyverse alternative

The most common reasons owners search for an alternative:

Add-on costs stack up. Loyverse's core POS is free, but capabilities like employee management and advanced inventory are paid monthly add-ons — billed per store. Add two or three and the "free" system has a real monthly bill, charged separately from each other.

Restaurant-first workflow. Owners often want the order-to-kitchen flow front and center — waiter takes the order, it lands on the kitchen screen instantly — rather than a general retail checkout.

Predictable pricing. One flat price for everything is easier to budget than a base app plus a menu of paid modules.

What to compare in a Loyverse alternative

Total monthly cost — not just the headline "free". Add up every module you actually need, per store.

Kitchen display — is a real-time kitchen display system included, or extra?

Offline reliability — does it keep selling when the internet drops, then sync automatically?

Devices included — is multi-device sync counted per device, per store, or unlimited?

Platforms — Android, iOS and Windows, so you're not boxed into one ecosystem.

How ServePoint compares

ServePoint takes the opposite approach to add-ons: one flat $5/month includes everything — order management, a real-time kitchen display, inventory, sales reports, thermal printing, QR menu and unlimited devices. There are no per-module fees and no per-transaction cut. It's built restaurant-first around the waiter-to-kitchen flow, it's offline-first, and it runs on Android, iOS and Windows.
